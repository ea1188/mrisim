Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 July 2026

This policy explains what data MRISim ("we", "the site") collects, why, and your choices. The free simulator, protocol planner, lessons and quiz work with no account and no personal data. Personal data is only involved if you create an account or buy the course.

What we collect

  • Account email. If you sign in (with Google or a one-time email link), we store your email address and a user id to identify your account and course access.
  • Learning progress. Which lessons you've read, quiz scores and mock-exam results are stored in your browser (localStorage). If you are enrolled in a class, activity you choose to record may be stored with your account.
  • Purchase records. If you buy the course, our payment provider processes the payment. We receive a confirmation and a reference id to grant your access. We never see or store your card details.

We do not run advertising trackers, and we do not sell personal data.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of processors, each with their own privacy terms:

  • Supabase — accounts, authentication and course data storage.
  • Stripe — payment processing for the paid course. Card data is handled entirely by Stripe.
  • Google — optional one-click sign-in (OAuth). Google shares your email and basic profile with us only when you choose to sign in with Google.
  • GitHub Pages — static website hosting.
  • jsDelivr — a public CDN that serves the in-browser physics runtime.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising cookies. The site uses browser localStorage to keep your learning progress and, when signed in, your authentication session. Clearing your browser storage removes this local data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, or to have your account and data deleted, email us at erolakkoc8@gmail.com and we will act on your request.

Data retention and security

We keep account and purchase data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the course and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymise it. Data is transmitted over HTTPS and access is protected by row-level security on our database.

Children

MRISim is intended for a professional and higher-education audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 where applicable). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email erolakkoc8@gmail.com.
