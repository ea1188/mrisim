About

Learn MRI by seeing the physics

MRISim is a browser-based MRI physics simulator and a guided course. It turns the equations behind an MR image into something you can watch respond, so the concepts stick.

What it is

Most MRI teaching is static: diagrams and formulas that never move. MRISim runs a real physics engine in your browser. Change the sequence, TR, TE, or flip angle and the reconstructed image updates the way a scanner's would, so you build intuition for how the parameters shape contrast, signal, resolution and scan time. There is nothing to install and no account needed to start.

Who it's for

  • Radiography and radiologic technology students learning MRI for the first time.
  • MRI technologists and radiographers preparing for registry exams.
  • Radiology residents, physicists and clinicians who want a feel for the parameter trade-offs.
  • Educators who want a live demonstration tool for the classroom.

How it fits together

The simulator, protocol planner, lessons and quiz are free and open to everyone, with no account. The guided course is a paid, structured path that adds premium lessons, a large registry-style question bank, mock exams and a searchable reference library, with your progress tracked as you go. The free tools are how you explore; the course is how you prepare.

Open source and open data

The MRISim software is open source under the MIT License. It renders real, openly licensed anatomy; the datasets and their licenses are listed on the credits page. It is an educational tool, not a medical device, and is not affiliated with any registry or certifying body.
