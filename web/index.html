Welcome to MRISim

The real MRI physics engine, running in your browser.

  • Sequence & timing — pick a sequence and sweep TR / TE / flip on the left panel.
  • Navigate — scroll the wheel or press ↑/↓ over the image to change slice; drag the image to window/level (R resets).
  • Compare — “Set as A”, then tweak B to see the difference side by side.
  • FOV planning — toggle the 3-plane localizer (F) and click a panel to reposition the slice.

This browser edition is a convenience subset. For the fullest, most robust experience — real body atlases, NIfTI/DICOM, the complete planning workflow and faster rendering — grab the desktop app.
