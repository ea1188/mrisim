Data sources & credits

MRISim's free simulator renders real, openly licensed anatomy. The datasets below power that tool; the guided course is separate original teaching material. Attribution and the terms each dataset is used under are listed here.

Anatomical datasets

BrainWeb Simulated Brain DatabaseResearch / educational use

McGill University Brain Imaging Centre. brainweb.bic.mni.mcgill.ca ↗

The digital brain phantom behind the free simulator's brain anatomy, remapped to tissue classes for signal simulation. Used for research and educational purposes.

KneeBones3Dify Annotated DatasetCC BY 4.0

Romano et al. Zenodo 10534328 ↗ (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.10534328), licensed CC BY 4.0.

A 3D isotropic T2 knee study. The free simulator's Knee region is a derived work: bone, soft-tissue and texture fields built from the annotated masks and real intensity.

SPIDER Lumbar Spine DatasetCC BY 4.0

van der Graaf et al., "SPIDER: Lumbar spine segmentation in MR images." Zenodo 10159290 ↗, licensed CC BY 4.0.

A sagittal lumbar T2 study. The free simulator's Spine region is a derived work: vertebrae, discs and spinal canal segmented, with the real T2 as texture.

TotalSegmentator MRI DatasetOpen (Zenodo)

Publicly available on Zenodo; underlying images sourced from the Imaging Data Commons.

Body regions (Abdomen, Pelvis, Torso) in the desktop simulator, built from combined organ masks and real-MRI texture. Not included in the browser build.

Course question images

Some course questions use clinical images to test image recognition. Simulator-rendered images are generated by MRISim itself. Any other clinical image is either owned by MRISim or openly licensed for commercial use (CC0, public domain, or CC BY); openly licensed images are credited beneath the image where they appear, with a link to the source. Images are shown resized. No non-commercial or education-only licensed images are used.
