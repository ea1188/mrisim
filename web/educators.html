What your students get

Real physics, real anatomy

Spin echo, gradient echo, IR, EPI, diffusion, perfusion, angiography and more — over real BrainWeb and segmented body atlases. K-space, artifacts, and live SNR / SAR / scan-time, so students see cause and effect, not canned pictures.

Nothing to install

Runs entirely in the browser on any laptop. Share a link to any exact setup — a specific TR/TE on a specific slice — so a demonstration drops straight into your slides or LMS.

Ready-made teaching

Guided lessons, a nine-module beginner curriculum, a scanner-console protocol planner, and an 80-question read-the-scan quiz with scored feedback and explanations — slot them in week by week.

Instructor tools with pilots

Cohorts, assignable exercises auto-graded against known-correct parameter ranges, and a progress dashboard — in development now, and shaped directly by our fall pilot partners.
