Real lumbar Spine region — SPIDER dataset
==========================================

Atlas + texture (atlas.npy, texture.npy) are processed by
scripts/build_spider_spine.py from one subject's T2 study of:

  J. van der Graaf et al., "SPIDER - Lumbar spine segmentation in MR images:
  a dataset and a public benchmark", Zenodo, https://zenodo.org/records/10159290
  License: CC-BY-4.0

Sagittal lumbar T1/T2 MRI with per-structure masks (vertebrae, intervertebral
discs, spinal canal). Only the small processed cache is committed here; the raw
3.7 GB image archive is not redistributed — the build script pulls a single
subject's image on demand via HTTP range requests.
