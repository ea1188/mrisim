Real Knee atlas — data source & attribution
============================================

The MRISim "Knee" region is built from:

  KneeBones3Dify-Annotated-Dataset (v1.0.0)
  Diego Romano et al.
  Zenodo: https://zenodo.org/records/10534328
  DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.10534328
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0)

A single 3-D isotropic T2 (3D SST2) right-knee MRI series (286 × 512 × 512 @
0.35 mm) with per-slice bone ground-truth masks.

The files in this directory (atlas.npy, texture.npy) are a DERIVED WORK produced
by scripts/build_knee_atlas.py: the bone masks split into cortical shell +
marrow, the surrounding soft tissue classified from the real T2 intensity into
subcutaneous fat / muscle / joint fluid / articular cartilage / skin, and the
real intensity normalised into a ~1.0 multiplicative texture field. Redistributed
here under CC-BY-4.0 with attribution to the original authors above.
