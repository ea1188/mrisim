# MRISim for instructors — quick start

MRISim is a free, browser-based MRI physics simulator (a real Python engine runs in
the student's browser — nothing to install). This page sets up a **class** so you can
share it with students and see their practice in one place. It takes about two minutes.

## 1. Sign in
Go to **https://ea1188.github.io/mrisim/account.html** → choose **Instructor**, enter
your school email, and click **Email me a sign-in link**. Open the email, click the
link, and you're in. (Passwordless — no account to remember; the link *is* your login.)

## 2. Create a class
On your dashboard, type a class name (e.g. *"MRI Physics — Fall 2026"*) and click
**Create class**. You'll get a **6-character join code**.

## 3. Share it with students
Give students two things:

- **The site:** https://ea1188.github.io/mrisim/
- **Their join step:** go to `…/account.html`, choose **Student**, sign in with their
  own email, and enter your **join code**.

Point them at whatever fits your lesson:

- **Simulator** — sweep TR/TE/flip, switch sequences and anatomy, watch k-space and
  quantitative maps update live.
- **Protocol planning** — a scanner-console workflow: prescribe each sequence on the
  scout images and acquire an exam.
- **Quiz** — read-the-scan self-test with scored feedback.

Once a student has joined your class, their quiz runs and completed lessons show up on
your dashboard automatically while they're signed in.

## 4. Watch progress
Your dashboard lists each class with its join code and a per-student table: quiz runs,
best score, lessons done, and last-active. Students who've joined but not started yet
show with no activity — handy for nudging.

**Archive** a class to tidy up your list (students can still see it); **Delete** removes
it and its data for good.

## Good to know
- **Practice, not grades.** Scores here are *formative* — for the student's own learning
  and your visibility, not a secure exam. (Trusted, proctored graded exams are a separate
  feature on the roadmap.)
- **The simulator is free and needs no account.** Accounts only add the class/roster/
  progress layer for instructors who want it.
- **Student data.** You're storing student names + emails + practice activity to run your
  course. Treat it as you would any class roster (see your institution's FERPA guidance).
